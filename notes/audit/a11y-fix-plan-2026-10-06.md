### Phase 4: Canvas geometry (one batch: stales steps 0–25, goldens regenerate)

**The approach: measure the DOM and hand it to the canvas.** Today each plot
group's floor comes from a hand-measured constant (`PLOT_RESERVE`, taken at
360px wide) and a `0.6h` floor. Each plot top comes from a hard-coded
`MARGIN+n` in six layouts. Neither sees the card or the title the reader
actually gets: 375px ([dirty] was 320px), 200% text, Safari's fonts, a title that wraps. Instead,
measure the tallest card and the tallest title among each group's steps, at the
live width. Hold both for as long as the width holds, so a step change inside a
group never moves the axes (motion rule 7). The canvas box itself stays where
it is: only the plot inside it moves.

1. **Measuring the cards** (`Step.svelte`, `Stage.svelte`):
   - Every `<Step>` in a plot group also renders its children a second time:
     an `inert`, `aria-hidden`, `visibility: hidden` copy, absolutely
     positioned inside `.scrolly-steps`. That gives it the live card's width
     and type, while staying out of `stepsHeight` and the accessibility tree.
     It reports its height through `bind:clientHeight`.
   - The intro steps (0–2) and the rank steps (6–7) are measured the same way.
     They become groups for this purpose, even though they draw no axes.
   - Controls in a card render a static form while measuring: a `measuring`
     context set by the copy. PairQuiz and StartButton skip their effects and
     `story` writes in that form, and PairQuiz shows its tallest state (both
     chips plus a verdict line).
   - Step 10's measured height adds the scrubber row, which docks on top of the
     card (M8).
   - Stage takes the max per group, plus the card's padding and the in-card
     cue row (Phase 3), and caps it at the card's `max-height` (D6). It
     publishes the result as `reserves`. Safe-area padding (D3), if it lands,
     is in the measured box automatically.
   - The measurement re-runs on width change, which already snaps the canvas,
     and on `document.fonts.ready`. A text-size change re-runs it too, as a
     snap: that is a change of box from the reader's side.
2. **Feeding the reserves to the canvas** (`plot.js`):
   - `PLOT_RESERVE` and `PLOT_BOTTOM_MIN` go. The stacked floor becomes
     `h - reserves[group] - AXIS_ROOM`. `shareBottomAt` stays for the sky's
     `fieldBox`.
   - The reserves reach the layouts through one setter, the way
     `setPlotBeside` does today. There is no default: a plot state does not
     draw until its group has been measured. The registry holds the step until
     then, which matters only on a dev `?step=N` load, since a fresh load
     measures at step 0.
   - The specs set reserves explicitly. The first commit sets them to today's
     constants, so the goldens stay byte-identical and the plumbing is proven
     before any geometry changes.
3. **M7**, the plot top:
   - ScrollyVisual measures each group's titles the same way, in a hidden copy
     of `.chart-title` at the title's width.
   - `plotTop(group)` in `plot.js` replaces the six hard-coded tops
     (`race.js:859`, `sim-race.js:104`, `scatters.js:46`, `career.js:143`,
     `hop-bands.js:160`, `rank.js:24`) and ScrollyVisual's `axisPlaces` /
     `titleOverrun`.
   - It is also exposed as `--plot-top`, which the RankBars panel top
     (`Stage.svelte:764`) and `.scrolly-steps.over` (Phase 3) read.
   - The title's font size becomes a token.
4. **M8**, steps 0–1:
   - the intro fit (`INTRO_BOTTOM_STACKED`, `intro-geometry.js`) gives way to
     the intro group's measured reserve plus the caption's height, so the
     caption gets its own band under the constellation;
   - `routeTop`'s clamp (`Index.svelte:142-146`) stops lifting the caption into
     the constellation;
   - step 2 is affected too, since `hopSeed` shares `introFrame`.
5. **Guards:**
   - a plot spec: for every group, the stacked floor plus `AXIS_ROOM` equals
     `h` minus the reserve, and the result does not depend on the step;
   - a contract that every state in a group reads the same reserve and plot
     top;
   - promote the review's overlap measurement (glyph boxes of the prose
     against ticks, axis titles and chart titles, from
     `sheets/a11y/verify/`) to a committed script beside `npm run a11y`. It
     runs at 375, 390 and 667×375, and at 200% text ([dirty] was 320, 375 and 667×375).
6. **'30 tick:** a new token, `chart.tick-horizon` (alpha 0.75, ≥4.5 against the
   page), with `FUTURE_TICK_HORIZON_ALPHA` (`race.js:1455`) set to match. A race
   spec asserts that they agree.
7. **`.hit` focus ring:** a new token, `control.focus-canvas` (white), plus a
   page-coloured halo shadow on `.hit:focus-visible`.
8. **B3 plumbing:**
   - export `simLabelText` (`sim-race.js:228`);
   - add `raceLeaderAt(year)` to `race.js`, from `story.eras`.
